A note for Santa's helper

A little prep.
A lot of magic.

One organiser can set everything up. There’s no need to hide clues, involve shop staff, or buy anything along the way. Each team sends a photo and a note at every stop; use the host dashboard to approve it or ask for a retry.

  1. Choose an easy-to-find public meeting point as Santa’s HQ and tell everyone where it is. It’s where you’ll start and finish.
  2. Choose one specific plaza entrance for card 8 and share it with all teams. Any public bench, shop window and festive or attractive public display will do for the other flexible stops.
  3. Check the four named venue signs before the day; if one has moved or closed, tell everyone a replacement. Agree on a finish time and share your phone number with team captains.
  4. Share the same website link with every team. Keep the host password private and stay online to review submissions. For performances, teams should send a representative photo and describe what they did.
  5. Bring a small treasure: chocolates, candy canes or a little wrapped gift. Awards for best team spirit and creativity are always welcome!
Good to know

Keep it merry & respectful.

Walk, don’t run, and stay with your team. Keep entrances and walkways clear, don’t move or attach anything to property, and avoid photographing strangers without permission. No purchases are required.
