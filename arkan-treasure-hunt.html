1. The Story (read this aloud at the start)

Long ago — well, last Tuesday — a time-traveling Sphynx cat named Cleocatra smuggled the legendary Golden Gelato Goblet of Arkan out of the pharaohs' freezer and hid it somewhere in the plaza. She left behind a trail of eighteen riddles, one at each of her favorite haunts, and a single warning scratched into a croissant:

"Solve my rhymes, earn your letters, spell my three words, and the goblet is yours — or you'll be stuck licking the fountain forever."

Act I gives you the first two words. The Nine Lives Gauntlet tests whether you deserve the rest. Act II gives you the last word — guarded by cat taxes, traps, and at least one suspiciously judgmental bakery spiral. String all eighteen letters together and they spell exactly what Cleocatra stole: GOLDEN GELATO GOBLET — which also tells you where she put it.

Five guilds of brave, slightly ridiculous explorers must race through Arkan Plaza, crack her clues, perform her absurd dares, and survive her tricks. May the gooiest team win. 🐱🍦

3. Rules & Scoring

  • Stay inside Arkan Plaza. Buddy system, hydrated, watch for cars in the garage and in the lanes. It's a hunt, not a heist. No running indoors — walk fast, strut even faster.
  • Each team carries a Team Sheet (Section 15), the Trail pages (riddles only — letters are sealed at each stop), and a Treasure Pouch for collected letters.
  • Route: everyone does stops 1 → 18 in order, staggered releases. After stop 12 → the Nine Lives Gauntlet → Act II (stops 13–18) → RUN back to Stop 1 to finish.
  • At every stop, solve the riddle (say the venue aloud to the host / open the sealed flap on the posted Stop Card), do the dare, get host initials. No initials = no points, no letter.
  • Photos are proof. Most dares require a team photo. Keep your camera roll ready and show the host.
  • No Googling Cleocatra. Using the internet to solve a riddle = −3 per offense (Curse card 🌐). Ask a human, ask a shop, ask a pigeon.
  • Hint tokens: each team gets 1 free "Cleocatra Whisker" hint (Emerald Ibises get 2). Extra hints cost −2 each, declared out loud so everyone judges you lovingly.
  • First team back at the courtyard with all 18 letters spelling the three words correctly gets the Finish Bonus.

4. Host Setup Checklist (do this before the teams arrive)

  • Print 18 Stop Cards (Section 16) on card stock; fold each letter under the gold flap; tape one at each venue (ask staff politely first — a tip or a treat for the café helps).
  • Print one Team Sheet (Section 15) per team + copies of the Trail pages (Sections 5 & 7) for every team.
  • Hide 3 Cleocatra paw-print stickers around the plaza for Side Quest S3 (plaza staff-approved spots only).
  • Cut the Twist Deck (10), Curse Deck (6), and 6 Gauntlet cards (Section 6) into shuffled decks.
  • Prepare 5 bandanas, a whistle, a timer, 1 "free hint" token per team (2 for Green), a marker for initials, a fully charged phone for scoreboard photos.
  • Stage the Gauntlet Hub by the open courtyard by the cinema (Cima Arkan): water, a roll of toilet paper (mummy wrap), a ball + stick (scarab roll).
  • Freeze the trophy bag: yes, literally put the trophy/prize envelope in a freezer bag with ice — Cleocatra kept it frozen. 🧊
  • Agree with one gelato/chocolate counter (Stavolta Gelato or Ladurée) if you want the finale to end at the counter instead of the courtyard.
  • Tell everyone the safety word: if any host shouts "SPHYNX!", all teams freeze and listen.

6. The Nine Lives Gauntlet MANDATORY · BETWEEN ACTS

⚔️ How it works

At the Gauntlet Hub (open courtyard by the cinema (Cima Arkan)), each team draws one face-down challenge card and has 2 minutes on the clock. Complete it for +10 — and +3 speed bonus if the host's timer says under 2:00. Fail and you may redraw once (0 points, maximum dignity lost). Then hand over your Act I letters: the host staples/clips a sealed Act II map envelope to your sheet. Only then do stops 13–18 unlock.

🃏 Card 1 — Mummy Wrap Race: Wrap one teammate in toilet paper from head to toe in 90 seconds. They must shamble three steps and say "I wake… for gelato."
🃏 Card 2 — The Sphinx's Riddle: The host asks ONE riddle (answers in the Key). Correct = pass. Wrong = you must hiss at your own shadow for 10 seconds, then try again.
🃏 Card 3 — Scarab Roll: Roll a ball (or a fruit) across the courtyard to a line and back using only a stick — no hands. Dropping it = start over with a dramatic scarab death pose first.
🃏 Card 4 — Canopic Jar Quiz: Five rapid-fire questions from the host (Key §G). Need 4 of 5. Shouting answers in unison encouraged.
🃏 Card 5 — Cleocatra's Catwalk: Strut a 5-meter catwalk to the host's beat (they hum), ending in a pose + one perfect purr into the camera.
🃏 Card 6 — The Sacred Meow Choir: The whole team sings their team chant — but every word is "meow." The host must be seen wiping away a tear.

9. Twist Deck (host draws these mid-hunt, shuffled — play them often, play them mean, play them silly)

🧊 Mummy Freeze: When the host blows a whistle, EVERYONE freezes like a wrapped mummy for 20 seconds. Last to freeze loses 2 pts; best freeze earns 3 pts.
👑 Pharaoh's Swap: Two randomly chosen teams must swap one non-essential item (a hat, a phone case, a shoe) and keep it until the finish. Returning it intact = +2 each.
🐱 Sphinx Ambush: A roving "Sphinx" (the Host in sunglasses) stops a team and asks a silly riddle, e.g. "What has keys but opens no door, and is full of beans at Arkan?" (Answer: a piano / Coffee Berry). Correct = +2.
🤳 Selfie Safari: Three hidden bonus selfie spots (e.g. under the prettiest night-light arch, by the biggest plant, on a red bench). First team to show all 3 = +5.
🍦 The Stavolta Decoy: At Stop 3, the Host may "lose" the letter and make the team do 5 jumping jacks to "melt the gelato" and recover it. Pure chaos, 0 points, maximum joy.
🐪 Camel Stampede: Host shouts "CAMEL!" — every nearby team drops to their knees and poses as a camel (knee-walk, confused blink). Most committed camel = +3; giggling before the pose = −1 (gentle).
🧶 Cleocatra's Hairball: The host secretly hands one team a crumpled gold "hairball." It's worth +4 at the finish — but if another team photographs it in their possession, they may shout "HAIRBALL THEFT!" and steal it (+4 for them, −4 for you). Keep it hidden. Misery loves company.
purr… purr… 🎵 The Sacred Purr: The team must purr in unison for 10 full seconds to a bystander's satisfaction. The bystander is the judge. Approval = +2; bystander laughing too hard to judge = +1 anyway, you adorable disasters.
🥐 Anti-Gravity Bakery: The team must balance a coin (or a folded note) on their forehead and walk 20 meters to the next checkpoint. Success = +2; dropping it = theatrical slow-motion collapse, −0 (but the replay is priceless).
🗣️ The Tourist Trap: The team must teach a willing bystander their full chant, complete with motions. If the bystander performs it for the camera = +3. If the bystander performs it better than the team = +3 and the team buys them a compliment.

10. Curse Deck (host draws when someone breaks a rule, or when chaos needs seasoning — may be dodged by the Scarab's Shield)

🌐 Google Shame: Caught Googling a riddle = −3, and the whole team must announce "WE TRIED TO CHEAT" to the nearest café queue.
😼 Meow Law: Until your next stop, you may only communicate with hosts in meows. Slipping into words = −2. (Ordering food counts as communication. Good luck.)
🗿 Pharaoh's Silence: No talking at all for 5 minutes — only dramatic hand gestures. A word = −3.
🎺 Wrong Chant: Performing another team's chant at a checkpoint = −2 and you must adopt their mascot pose for one photo.
🙅 Dare Dodge: Refusing a printed dare (safety and dignity exceptions apply) = −5. Cleocatra sees all.
🏃 Indoor Sprint: Running indoors, in the garage, or through staff-only doors = −3 and a safety lecture delivered in rhyme by the host.
