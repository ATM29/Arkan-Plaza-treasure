1. The Story (read this aloud at the start)

Long ago — well, last Tuesday — a time-traveling Sphynx cat named Cleocatra smuggled the legendary Golden Gelato Goblet of Arkan out of the pharaohs' freezer and hid it somewhere in the plaza. She left behind a trail of twelve riddles, one at each of her favorite haunts, and a single warning scratched into a croissant:

"Solve my rhymes, earn your letters, spell the treat, and the goblet is yours — or you'll be stuck licking the fountain forever."

Five guilds of brave, slightly ridiculous explorers must race through Arkan Plaza, crack her clues, perform her absurd dares, and collect twelve letters. String them together and they spell where the goblet waits. May the gooiest team win. 🐱🍦

2. Meet the 5 Teams

Give each team a colored bandana/ribbon, a silly name, a mascot god, and a chant they must perform whenever they pass a Host Checkpoint.

BLUE The Sapphire Scarabs

Patron god: Khepri, the dawn beetle who rolls the sun across the sky (and, allegedly, the goblet across the plaza).

Chant: "Roll it, roll it, scarab style — we shine bluer than the Nile!"

RED The Crimson Cobras

Patron god: Wadjet, the cobra goddess who protects with a hiss and a hairflip.

Chant: "Hiss, snap, strike — red cobras never strike out!" (add a dramatic snake motion)

GREEN The Emerald Ibises

Patron god: Thoth, the ibis-headed god of wisdom, writing, and questionable dance moves.

Chant: "Quill and beak, we're super wise — Emerald Ibises win the prize!"

GOLD The Golden Jackals

Patron god: Anubis, the jackal who guides souls — and, today, confused treasure hunters.

Chant: "Anubis guide, jackals gold — we sniff out treats like legends told!"

PURPLE The Violet Vultures

Patron god: Nekhbet, the vulture who circles protectively (and eyes your fries).

Chant: "Circle high, swoop down low — Violet Vultures steal the show!" (flap arms)

3. Rules & Scoring

  • Stay inside Arkan Plaza. Buddy system, hydrated, watch for cars in the garage. It's a hunt, not a heist.
  • Each team carries a Clue Sheet (Section 9) and a Treasure Pouch (any small bag) for collected letters/trinkets.
  • At every stop a Host (or a posted clue card) confirms the riddle answer and initials the task. No initials = no points.
  • Photos are proof. Most tasks require a team photo. Keep your camera roll ready.
  • No Googling Cleocatra. Using the internet to solve a riddle = −3 points per offense. Ask a human, ask a shop, ask a pigeon.
  • First team back at the fountain with all 12 letters spelled correctly gets the Finish Bonus.
ActionPoints
Correct riddle answer at a stop (host initials)+5
Completed dare / photo at a stop+5
Bonus micro-dare at a stop (★)+1 to +2
Quirky Twist cards (Section 6)−2 to +5
Finish bonus (1st team back, letters correct)+10
Internet cheating−3 each

Max ~120+ before twists. Tie-breaker: best team chant, judged by a random bystander.

4. The 12-Stop Trail

Teams proceed in numerical order, wrapping from 12 back to 1. Each stop gives one letter. In order the letters spell GOLDENGELATO → "Golden Gelato" → the goblet is frozen gold, hidden where the chocolate drips. (Final reveal at the fountain.)

G  STOP 1 — The Grand Fountain (Heart of Arkan)

"I am the pulse where Arkan's veins all meet,
where water leaps and strangers greet.
Stand where the plaza's heartbeat sounds —
the first G is where your quest is found."
Dare: Huddle-photo with the fountain; everyone points at the water like they just discovered it.
★ Bonus: Name the fountain something absurd (e.g. "Lake Cleocatra") and shout it 3× together. (+1)

O  STOP 2 — Paul (The French Bakery & Café)

"From Paris I came with butter and pride,
my croissants flake on the Egyptian side.
Find the café where the Eiffel meets the Nile —
the letter O waits beneath its style."
Dare: Photo of everyone mime-eating an invisible croissant with maximum fancy.
★ Bonus: Say "Bonjour Arkan!" in a terrible French accent. (+1)

L  STOP 3 — Dipndip (The Chocolate Lab)

"I dip, I drip, I melt with glee,
chocolate crepes are the key to me.
Sweet and silly, gooey and gold —
the letter L in the cocoa is told."
Dare: Freeze like a chocolate-fountain statue (everyone strikes a pose, hold 10s) — photo.
★ Bonus: One member licks the air like it's a giant dipping strawberry. (+1)

D  STOP 4 — The Cinema

"Lights go low and the giants appear,
in 3D and VIP I bring the cheer.
Roll the reel where the stories play —
the letter D is just a stride away."
Dare: Red-carpet pose photo at the cinema entrance; one member announces the team in a deep movie-trailer voice: "In a plaza far, far away… THE SAPPHIRE SCARABS!"
★ Bonus: Entire team does the "popcorn explosion" jump. (+1)

E  STOP 5 — Kazoku (Japanese / Asian)

"From the land where the sun climbs high,
I roll my rice and let flavors fly.
Find the spot with the rising sun —
the letter E is the lucky one."
Dare: Bow to each other like samurai; synchronized "konnichiwa" wave — photo.
★ Bonus: One member says a Japanese food word correctly (sushi / ramen / matcha). (+1)

N  STOP 6 — Zooba (Egyptian Street Food)

"Taameya, koshari, foul — my street food makes the hungry whole.
Most Egyptian of all the plaza's fare —
the letter N is waiting there."
Dare: Photo of the team doing the "baladi bounce" (a silly local shuffle dance).
★ Bonus: Shout "Yalla!" like you mean it, then translate it. (+1)

G  STOP 7 — The Gourmet Food Market

"Cheese and olives, nuts and wine,
a market fine and oh-so-divine.
Where foodies gather to graze and gawk —
the letter G is the tasty talk."
Dare: Each member finds & photos ONE weird/luxury food item (e.g. truffle, fancy cheese, exotic fruit).
★ Bonus: Correctly name another team's weird item. (+2)

E  STOP 8 — Crowne Plaza Hotel (IHG)

"A crown of rest where travelers stay,
IHG's tower guards the day.
Bow to the king at the plaza's door —
the letter E is the royal score."
Dare: Team photo doing a royal bow/curtsy at the hotel entrance.
★ Bonus: One member gives a 10-second "Pharaoh of Arkan" acceptance speech. (+2)

L  STOP 9 — Jones the Grocer (Australian Gourmet)

"Down-under baker with a grocer's heart,
fresh bread and deli are my fine art.
Find the Aussie with the savory smell —
the letter L in the bakery dwell."
Dare: Photo of the team smelling bread dramatically (eyes closed, blissful).
★ Bonus: Attempt an Australian accent: "G'day, one meat pie, mate." (+1)

A  STOP 10 — The Arcade

"Beeps and blips and tickets to win,
I light up the night with a playful grin.
Where the gamers and kids convene —
the letter A is the in-between."
Dare: Team photo, everyone mid-"game face" (tongue out / focused squint).
★ Bonus: One member does a victory dance like they just beat the high score. (+1)

T  STOP 11 — Coffee Berry

"Bean and brew to wake the soul,
I keep the tired explorers whole.
Find the berry that fuels the quest —
the letter T is the caffeinated test."
Dare: Group photo toasting with empty "invisible coffee cups."
★ Bonus: Everyone says their order in one synchronized chant. (+1)

O  STOP 12 — Lucille's Diner (American)

"Pancakes stacked to the desert sky,
diner style with a wink and a sigh.
Last stop, y'all, before you're through —
the letter O is the final clue."
Dare: Team photo doing a diner "whistle for the waitress" pose.
★ Bonus: One member does the best fake-American "Hey darlin', refill please!" (+1)

FINAL RIDDLE (read aloud here): "You've spelled my treat from G to O — now hurry back where fountains flow. The Golden Gelato Goblet waits, where Arkan's heartbeat first took flight. RUN to STOP 1!"

6. Quirky Twists (shuffle these in mid-hunt)

🧊 Mummy Freeze: When the Host blows a whistle, EVERYONE freezes like a wrapped mummy for 20 seconds. Last to freeze loses 2 pts; best freeze earns 3 pts.
👑 Pharaoh's Swap: Two randomly chosen teams must swap one non-essential item (a hat, a phone case, a shoe) and keep it until the finish. Returning it = +2 each.
🐱 Sphinx Ambush: A roving "Sphinx" (the Host in sunglasses) stops a team and asks a silly riddle, e.g. "What has keys but opens no door, and is full of beans at Arkan?" (Answer: a piano / Coffee Berry). Correct = +2.
🤳 Selfie Safari: Three hidden bonus selfie spots (e.g. under the prettiest night-light arch, by the biggest plant, on a red bench). First team to post all 3 = +5.
🍫 The Dipndip Decoy: At Stop 3, the Host may "lose" the letter and make the team do 5 jumping jacks to "melt the chocolate" and recover it. Pure chaos, 0 points, maximum joy.

7. Finale & Prizes

When a team returns to Stop 1 (the Fountain) with all 12 letters, the Host checks the spelling: G · O · L · D · E · N · G · E · L · A · T · O.

Cleocatra's decree is read: "Golden Gelato! The goblet was never lost — it was the hunt itself. But winners get the real thing." Hand the champions the Golden Gelato Goblet (a trophy + Dipndip gelato for all) and the last-place team receives "Cleocatra's Curse" — a rubber chicken and the honor of planning next year's hunt.

Prize ideas: engraved "Golden Gelato Goblet" trophy 🏆 • Dipndip chocolate vouchers 🍫 • team-color crowns 👑 • a group photo with the fountain lit up at night 📸.
